Governance & Inquiries

Frequently asked questions.

Essential information on accessing our private sanctuaries and booking expeditions.

Depending on the terrain, guests are transferred via our private seaplanes, twin-turbine helicopters, or bespoke AWD overland rovers. A dedicated Wild expedition ranger guides your party directly from the nearest regional private airfield to your sanctuary dock.

Every sanctuary is equipped with real-time biometric telemetry, automated external defibrillators (AED), hyperbaric emergency chambers (at high-altitude aeries), and satellite SOS links with 24/7 airborne medevac extraction coverage.

Yes. Qualified drivers complete a comprehensive 90-minute all-terrain telemetry orientation at our dispatch base. We also provide on-demand professional wilderness pilots if you prefer to relax and enjoy the landscape.

All sanctuaries utilize closed-loop bio-filtration, non-invasive ground anchors without concrete foundations, solar solid-state micro-grids, and zero-emission electric transportation. When a pod is relocated, the ground beneath remains 100% undisturbed.

If extreme meteorological conditions prevent safe airborne transfer, Wild provides complimentary luxury lodging at our regional basecamp and flexible priority re-routing to an alternate sanctuary within our global network.
